A farming auto-game which plays inside a fake vintage OS trough its interface.
Implemented in Godot 4.
Repository: https://github.com/ermolinaj/FarmOS

You receive a pack of seeds. You drag them into folders, they grow.
You collect them and drag their icons into a selling basket.
